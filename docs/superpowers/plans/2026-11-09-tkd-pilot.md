**Goal:** Ship a reviewable one-school demo with verified 2026 CBTKD age/rank labels, sourced public videos, and private practitioner accounts.

**Architecture:** Keep the Expo app and static teaching catalog. Add pure domain functions for age/rank logic and a small Supabase-backed account boundary. Protect account data in Postgres with row-level security and server-side invite-code validation.

**Tech Stack:** Expo 54, React Native 0.81, TypeScript 5.9, Node 26 test runner, Supabase Auth/Postgres.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-tkd-product-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- One school and an invite code; do not embed the code or a service-role key in the app.
- CBTKD 2026 competition rules establish age divisions and rank colors, not the instructor's curriculum.
- Public videos are labeled as demos and retain source attribution; no downloads or offline cache.
- Under-18 account profiles are guardian-owned; production onboarding requires privacy review.
- No in-app billing, instructor dashboard, multi-school tenancy, or grading workflow in this pilot.

## Review Focus

- Birth year outside the valid range must not produce a believable age division (Task 1 test).
- Age 17 and 31 must show all overlapping official divisions, with Sub-21 limited to Dan (Task 1 test).
- Every displayed video must have a known title and publisher, and a missing video must show an honest state (Task 2 test).
- Invalid invite codes must never create school membership; another account must not see private rows (Task 3 SQL policy review and tests when Supabase is available).
- Switching accounts must not expose the previous user's favorites or profile (Task 4 test and manual smoke).
